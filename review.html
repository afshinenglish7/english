Gate 5 · Now Boarding

🗣️ Partner Interview — Round 1

Work in pairs. Student A asks Student B the questions below, then switch.

Student A asks:

  1. What's your name, and how do you spell your last name?
  2. Where are you from? What's your nationality?
  3. Are you a student, or do you work?
  4. What's your phone number?
  5. What's in your bag today? Name three things.
  6. What day is your favorite day of the week? Why?

Student B asks:

  1. How old are you? (You can say "I'd rather not say"!)
  2. Who's your best friend? Where's he/she from?
  3. Is your city big or small?
  4. How much is your phone, more or less?
  5. Point to something in the room. "What's that?"
  6. Are you a good cook? A good singer? A good dancer?

🗣️ Partner Interview — Round 2: Things & Numbers

Switch partners if you can. Ask and answer these too.

Ask about things:

  1. What's your favorite souvenir? Where's it from?
  2. Is this pen expensive or cheap?
  3. How many keys are on your key chain?
  4. Do you have sunglasses? Are they new or old?
  5. What's one thing you never leave home without?

Ask about numbers:

  1. What's your address (just the street number)?
  2. What's your email address? Spell it!
  3. How many students are in this class?
  4. What time is it right now?
  5. What's a good price for a cup of coffee, in your opinion?

🗣️ Partner Interview — Round 3: Family, Routine & Free Time

A third round for units 4–8. Switch partners again if you can.

Ask about family & abilities:

  1. Do you have brothers or sisters? What are their names?
  2. What does your mother or father do (job)?
  3. Can you cook? Can you swim? Can you drive?
  4. What's one thing you can do really well?
  5. Is your family big or small?

Ask about routine & free time:

  1. What time do you usually get up on weekdays?
  2. What do you usually do on weekends?
  3. What do you like doing in your free time?
  4. What's your favorite kind of movie? Why?
  5. Do you love or hate cooking? Cleaning?

🎯 Quick-Fire: 6 Topics, 30 Seconds Each

🔎 Find Someone Who... (Mingle Bingo)

Stand up! Ask your classmates questions. When someone answers "yes," write their name in the box. Try to fill the whole grid.

🎲 Two Truths and a Lie

Write 3 sentences about yourself: two true, one false. Use the verb "be" (e.g., nationality, job, age, a possession). Read them to your group — can they guess the lie?

📊 Class Survey

Ask 5 classmates a question of your choice (e.g., "Are you from this city?" / "Do you have a cell phone?"). Tally the answers.

📖 Storytelling Chain

How to play: Sit in a circle. Student 1 says one sentence about a traveler using target grammar (e.g., "Her name is Elena. She's from Poland."). Student 2 repeats it and adds one sentence. Continue around the group — how long can the story get without a mistake?

🎤 My Passport — 30-Second Presentation

Prepare, then present to the class or a small group. Include: your name and how to spell it, your nationality, your job, one small thing in your bag, and one question for the audience.
